# Code of conduct

[Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

SeamlessControl welcomes users and contributors regardless of experience, identity, background or preferred language. These standards apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing ideas, code and problems.
- Assume good intent, accept corrections and make room for different levels of experience.
- Keep reports and examples free of private keys, credentials, personal data and unredacted device or network details.
- Credit other people's work and respect their boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and repeated disruption are not accepted.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a private conduct report, email [soporte@asistentesautonomos.com](mailto:soporte@asistentesautonomos.com) with the relevant links, dates and a short description. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. If the report concerns the maintainer or GitHub-hosted conduct, you may also use [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer will review reports discreetly, hear relevant parties where appropriate and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a temporary pause or a ban from project spaces. Information will be shared only as needed to address the report. Retaliation against a reporter is not accepted.
